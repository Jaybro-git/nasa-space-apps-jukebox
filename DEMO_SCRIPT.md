# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

**0:00 — Eyes closed.**
"Close your eyes. Can you hear the planet change?"
- Press **Enter** on the start screen: the voice guide introduces the app, then **Ocean Currents & Sea Surface Temperature**, playing its lowest and highest notes.
- Press **Space**. "One note per data sample. Higher means a warmer ocean. Nothing else has a beat: any rhythm you hear is the data."

**0:25 — Rhythm is data.**
- Switch Style to **Detailed**: "Now you also hear the exact value as a quiet gliding tone, and a flute on the right when the ocean jumps warmer."
- Point out the bell at each new month.

**0:40 — Freeze and explore.**
- Press **E**. "The picture stops so you can study it."
- Arrow keys: "14 degrees north, 140 degrees west: 28.4 °C."
- Press **2**, then **Enter**: a sweep west → east along the equator, with ticks every 30°. Press **4**, **Enter**: a box scan, then the spoken summary. **]** steps to the next frame. **Esc** resumes.

**1:05 — Lightning.**
- Press **N** twice for **Lightning**. "Every flash seen from the ISS is a plucked hit where it strikes. The rhythm is the lightning."

**1:20 — Aerosols and the Earth.**
- Pick **Aerosols** from the dropdown: "Smoke, dust, sea salt and sulfate on one day." Box tool, **A**, **Enter**: the whole planet scanned west → east, and "Mostly dust, then sulfate".
- Pick **Earth: Countries**, zoom with **+**: borders and names. Move the probe: tick, "France". Tick, "Spain".

**1:45 — Night mode and access.**
- Press **M**: the real Milky Way behind the app.
- "Keyboard only, a voice guide you can switch off, screen-reader friendly, no autoplay."

**2:00 — Close.**
"Real NASA data, musical by design, built for people who listen."
